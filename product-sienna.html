Color Accuracy & Product Disclaimer

We strive to display product colors as accurately as possible. However, actual colors may vary slightly due to fabric dye lots, weather conditions during dyeing, studio lighting, and individual monitor or screen settings.

Handcrafted Fabrics

Hand-woven textiles naturally feature minor variations, including darker threads or weave irregularities. These are not defects, but inherent characteristics that showcase the raw beauty and authenticity of handcrafted items.

Sizing Tolerance

Please note that due to the manual nature of our production, a size variation of +/- 2 inches is considered standard and acceptable.

Exchanges are accepted only for manufacturing defects. We do not offer exchanges or returns for change of mind, incorrect size selection, or general dissatisfaction.

To request an exchange, contact us within 7 days of delivery with your proof of purchase and clear photos of the defect. Items must be unused and in their original packaging with tags intact. Defects from wear and tear, misuse, or improper care are not covered.
